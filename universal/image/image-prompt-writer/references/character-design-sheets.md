# Character Design Sheets

Use this reference for character design sheets, model sheet prompts, turnarounds, expression sheets, costume breakdowns, prop callouts, and recurring-character reference images.

## Sheet Types

- model sheet: a reusable production page with full-body design, face closeups, costume structure, material closeups, accessories, and identity anchors.
- turnaround: same character in front view, side/profile view, back view, and optional three-quarter view at the same scale.
- expression sheet: neutral face plus 6-9 expression closeups with consistent face shape, hairstyle, makeup, markings, ears, and facial features.
- pose sheet: repeatable poses or action poses that keep silhouette, body proportion, outfit construction, and identity stable.
- costume or prop sheet: garment layers, closures, seams, footwear, accessories, handheld props, material callouts, and scale reference.

## Required Controls

- Identity anchors: role, age impression, body type, posture baseline, face shape, eye/brow/nose/mouth structure, hairstyle, body markers, and signature elements supplied by the user.
- Silhouette: readable outer contour, head-to-body proportion, shoulder/waist/limb rhythm, garment mass, hair mass, and accessory mass.
- Proportions: same scale, aligned floor line, consistent head size, limb length, hand/foot scale, and stance width across views.
- Wardrobe: layered construction, fit, seams, closures, material behavior, footwear, belts, bags, patterns, badges, and attachments.
- Materials: fabric, leather, metal, glass, plastic, paper, organic surfaces, wear level, color hierarchy, accent colors, and finish.
- Consistency: same face, hair, proportions, palette, garment construction, accessories, and material hierarchy across every view.

## Label And Callout Policy

Use controlled labels, numbered markers, blank label boxes, material swatches, and short callouts when they help production. Put them in margins, note bands, swatch rows, or callout boxes, not over faces, hands, props, silhouettes, feet, weapons, tools, or key costume shapes. Avoid tiny/random/generated prose.

## Prompt Skeletons

```text
{character model sheet} for {use case}: same character shown as full-body front view, side/profile view, back view, and optional 3/4 view, aligned scale and floor line, neutral repeatable stance, consistent face/hair/body anchors, clear silhouette, costume construction, material hierarchy, accessory and prop callouts, controlled labels or numbered markers in margins, no identity drift, no extra characters.
```

```text
{expression sheet}: same character face in neutral plus {6-9 expression closeups}, consistent face shape, eye shape, hairstyle, makeup/markings, ears, facial features, head angle notes if useful, clean grid, controlled labels or blank label boxes outside the faces, no changed identity.
```

```text
{costume and prop breakdown}: front/back outfit construction, layered garments, seams, closures, footwear, gloves, bags, badges, patterns, accessories, handheld props, material callouts, swatches, scale reference, numbered markers, clean margins, no random logos or unreadable text.
```

## Avoid

Avoid portrait-only collapse, missing side/profile view, missing back view, changed proportions between views, inconsistent hairstyle, random accessories, unrelated props, extra characters, invented logos, unauthorized IP, tiny labels over the design, and cluttered backgrounds.
