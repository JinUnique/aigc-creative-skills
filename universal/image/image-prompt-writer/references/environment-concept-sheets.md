# Environment Concept Sheets

Use this reference for environment concept sheet prompts, production-design boards, interiors, exteriors, explorable spaces, location references, background plates, and spatial design views.

## Sheet Types

- environment concept sheet: one page showing a hero concept view plus supporting design views.
- production-design board: spatial layout, function, pathing, props, materials, light logic, and state variants for a location.
- background plate: single frame or board where subject staging must remain clear in front of the environment.
- location callout sheet: detail callouts for doors, windows, signage shapes, fixtures, props, joints, trims, material transitions, and surface wear.

## Required Controls

- Spatial layout: entrance, exit, focal zone, circulation loop, primary path, secondary paths, blocked areas, thresholds, and vertical levels.
- View plan: hero concept view, top-down plan-like view, alternate angle, elevation-like support, section-like support, and detail closeups when useful.
- Scale: human silhouettes, doors, stairs, furniture, vehicles, plants, tiles, tools, railings, scale bars, or other scale references.
- Function: what the space is for, who uses it, how it is navigated, which zones are public/private/service/rest/work/display/action areas.
- Material system: structural material, surface finish, wear logic, weathering, modular pieces, swatches, and value hierarchy.
- Lighting/color: motivated window, practical, overhead, bounce, sun, sky, emergency, display, or work light; color script and atmosphere that preserve layout readability.

## Labels, Legends, And Signage

Controlled labels, legends, scale bars, arrows, numbered markers, material swatches, and callouts are useful on environment sheets. Put them in margins, note bands, plan legends, or callout boxes. Distinguish unauthorized/random signage, random brands, and fake place names from controlled labels that explain the design. If exact signage is unreliable or not authorized, use blank sign panels or numbered markers.

## Prompt Skeletons

```text
{environment concept sheet}: large 3/4 hero concept view showing entrance, focal zone, paths, scale cues, and narrative function; top-down plan-like view showing entrances, exits, circulation, key areas, and vertical levels; alternate angle; elevation-like support or section-like support; material/color swatches; controlled labels, legends, scale bars, or numbered markers in margins; no unauthorized/random signage or brands.
```

```text
{single environment frame}: designed space for {use case}, clear foreground entry, midground function, background boundary, visible path, focal zone, scale cues, material system, motivated lighting, atmosphere that does not hide layout, optional blank signage or controlled labels only when requested.
```

```text
{reference edit}: Preserve: existing layout, main focal zone, camera angle, lighting direction, material identity. Edit: {specific zone, prop, weather, state, or time change}. Avoid: changed geography, blocked path, broken scale, random signage, unreadable function, new brands.
```

## Avoid

Avoid unclear paths, impossible perspective, broken scale, decorative clutter, random people, overgrown details hiding layout, unauthorized/random signage, invented logos, fake place names, and labels covering key environment shapes.
