# Photography And Cinematic Frames

Use this reference for photography prompts, portraits, headshots, lifestyle images, landscapes, ordinary single frame images, cinematic frame prompts, keyframes, action stills, and explicit text-free image requests.

## Ordinary Single Frame

For an ordinary single frame, prioritize one clear subject, use case, aspect ratio, composition, environment, camera/medium, lighting/color, material/texture, and constraints. Do not force a design sheet, labels, or storyboard structure unless the user asks for them.

## Photography Controls

- Subject: identity or category, expression, pose, wardrobe, product relation, body crop, and what must remain visible.
- Camera: lens feel, viewpoint, crop, depth of field, shutter feel for action, and subject distance.
- Light: soft window, studio, practical, sun, overcast, backlight, rim, bounce, motivated cinematic source, or product light.
- Texture: skin texture, fabric weave, hair detail, terrain, water, product finish, grain, haze, or sharpness.
- Use case: avatar, editorial, lifestyle, catalog, documentary, travel, social post, reference image, or background plate.

## Cinematic Frame And Keyframe Controls

- Narrative beat: one readable moment, not a whole sequence.
- Staging: subject placement, eyeline, prop state, foreground/midground/background, and environmental story cues.
- Camera/medium: wide, medium, close, insert, profile, over-shoulder, high/low angle, lens, and frame shape.
- Lighting/color: motivated source, contrast, palette, atmospheric depth, and value separation.
- Action readability: visible silhouette, hands/feet/contact point, force direction, and minimal blur when action matters.

## Explicit Text-Free Image

When the user requests an explicit text-free image, include: no visible lettering, numbers, labels, captions, signs, UI text, watermarks, signatures, logos, or typography-like marks. Replace text needs with visual symbols, color coding, composition, blank surfaces, or post-production space.

## Prompt Skeletons

```text
{photography prompt}: {subject} for {use case}, {composition/aspect ratio/crop}, {pose/action/expression}, {environment}, {camera/lens/viewpoint/depth}, {lighting/color}, {material/skin/fabric/terrain/product texture}, visible text plan: {text-free / requested prop text / clean negative space}, avoid {artifacts and drift}.
```

```text
{cinematic frame}: ordinary single frame showing {one narrative beat}, subject placement and eyeline, prop or action state, foreground/midground/background staging, motivated lighting, color palette, lens and camera angle, atmosphere that preserves readability, no unintended captions or logos.
```

```text
{action still}: {subject} frozen at {anticipation/contact/impact/follow-through}, clear silhouette, line of action, center of gravity, visible contact point, force direction, grounded shadow, minimal blur, no hidden hands/feet/contact point.
```
